# Blinkit-Analytics
Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard – Grocery Sales & Outlet Performance Analysis

Project Title / Headline

Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard | Grocery Sales, Outlet Performance & Customer Insights Using Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Blinkit’s sales performance, outlet distribution, item categories, customer ratings, and outlet trends to support better retail and operational decision-making.

Purpose

The purpose of this dashboard is to help business managers and retail teams monitor sales performance across outlet types, item categories, fat content, and outlet locations. It enables data-driven insights for improving product strategy, outlet efficiency, and overall business growth.

Tech Stack (Tools Used)
Power BI – Dashboard creation and interactive data visualization
Microsoft Excel / CSV – Data storage and preprocessing
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and custom measures
Data Source

The dataset used for this project is based on Blinkit grocery sales and outlet data containing information such as:

Item categories
Outlet establishment year
Outlet size and location type
Product fat content
Total sales and average sales
Customer ratings
Number of items sold
Outlet types and visibility

The dataset is structured to simulate real-world quick-commerce and retail analytics scenarios.

Features / Highlights
-Business Problem

Retail and quick-commerce businesses manage thousands of products across multiple outlet types and locations. Without centralized analytics, it becomes difficult to:

Track sales performance across outlets
Identify high-performing product categories
Understand customer preferences
Analyze outlet efficiency and visibility
Improve operational and inventory decisions

Businesses need a clear and interactive dashboard to convert raw sales data into actionable insights.

-Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal of this dashboard is to:

Monitor overall sales performance
Analyze product category contribution
Compare outlet performance across locations and sizes
Track customer ratings and average sales
Identify high-performing outlet types
Support business decisions with visual insights
Improve retail planning and inventory management
-Key Visuals & Insights
1. KPI Cards

The dashboard provides quick business performance indicators including:

Total Sales: $1.2M
Average Sales: $141
Number of Items: 8523
Average Rating: 3.9

These KPIs provide an instant overview of overall business performance.

2. Outlet Establishment Trend

This area chart shows sales trends based on outlet establishment years.

Insight:
Sales peaked around 2018 with approximately $205K.
After 2018, sales slightly declined but remained stable over time.
Older outlets continue contributing consistently to total revenue.
3. Fat Content Analysis

This donut chart compares sales generated by:

Low Fat products
Regular products
Insight:
Regular fat products contribute higher sales compared to low-fat products.
Customer demand is stronger for regular-category grocery products.
4. Item Type Performance

This horizontal bar chart analyzes sales across item categories such as:

Fruits & Vegetables
Snack Foods
Household Items
Frozen Foods
Dairy Products
Insight:
Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods generate the highest sales.
Seafood and Breakfast categories contribute the least revenue.
5. Outlet Size Analysis

This visualization compares sales across:

Small outlets
Medium outlets
High-size outlets
Insight:
Medium-sized outlets contribute the highest overall sales.
High-size outlets have comparatively lower contribution than expected.
6. Outlet Location Type Analysis

This funnel chart visualization compares Tier-wise outlet sales:

Tier 1
Tier 2
Tier 3
Insight:
Tier 3 locations generate the highest sales.
Tier 1 outlets show comparatively lower sales contribution.
7. Outlet Type Performance Table

This matrix visualization compares:

Total Sales
Number of Items
Average Sales
Average Rating
Item Visibility

Across outlet formats such as:

Grocery Stores
Supermarket Type 1
Supermarket Type 2
Supermarket Type 3
Insight:
Supermarket Type 1 contributes the highest total sales and item count.
Grocery stores have lower item counts but maintain competitive average ratings.
